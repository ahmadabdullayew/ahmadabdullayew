# Professional profile content review policy

**Review interval:** quarterly, and after any significant release, competition submission, research result, internship/education change, or link migration. **Current review baseline:** 2026-10-10 public evidence from Phase 3 (not a complete downstream software/benchmark audit).

## Review and update procedure

1. **Identity:** Confirm education/status, specialization and current opportunities directly with the profile owner. Do not infer completed degrees or employment.
2. **Featured work:** Reopen each linked repository; classify whether it remains a prototype, implementation foundation, completed empirical study, formal report or deployed service. Do not imply that source availability proves deployment.
3. **Quantitative results:** Report numerical metrics only with a cited date, dataset, exact command/output and verification provenance; distinguish project-reported results from independently reproduced ones. Remove superseded or unverifiable metrics rather than extrapolating.
4. **Authorship:** Confirm team roles and personal contributions with source-history or owner review. Owning a repository does not demonstrate personal authorship of every component.
5. **Technical inventory:** Keep skills linked to concrete source evidence, not badge collections. Mark research directions as ongoing until documented outcomes exist.
6. **Links and media:** Follow `LINK_REVIEW.md`; confirm linked demos, README anchors, evidence artifacts, mailto and professional contact destinations. Remote blocking is not automatically a 404.
7. **Activity graphics:** Treat the GitHub contribution snapshot as secondary; check that the summary and both responsive-theme pairs remain synchronized and static. Do not manually edit generator-owned outputs.
8. **Change control:** Update `README.md`, `CV.md`, `PROJECT_EVIDENCE.md`, `profile-spec.json` and relevant tests together when a shared claim or document contract changes; preserve history and note the review date.
9. **Quality gate:** Execute the validator, full unittest suite, and visual smoke tests. After publishing, perform the manual GitHub review in `VISUAL_REVIEW.md`.
